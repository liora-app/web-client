# Contour portrait geometry

The neutral head is derived from **Universal Base Characters (Standard)** by **Quaternius**.

- Original source: https://quaternius.com/packs/universalbasecharacters.html
- License: CC0 1.0 — https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/
- Modifications: extracted head/neck geometry, normalized coordinates, discarded skinning and textures.
- Original body: `Superhero_Female_FullBody.gltf`, Godot / UE export.

The contour shaders, light trails, animation, and composition are implemented in this project.
The mesh depicts a fictional stylized figure. No photographs or personal data are used.
